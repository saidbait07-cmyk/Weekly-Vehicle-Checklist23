Pre-Implementation Engineering Analysis & Production Blueprint (Section 29)

Complete checklist extraction from the attached Al-Ghalbi image, PostgreSQL 24-table DDL, offline synchronization protocol, and 30-case verification suite

1. Attached Al-Ghalbi Original Paper Checklist Reference

All 32 numbered rows, top header fields (Vehicle No, Starting Km, Type of Vehicle, Closing Km), and Sunday–Saturday columns have been digitized into Template v1.0.

2. Offline PWA & Idempotent Synchronization Architecture

  • Local Cache & Storage: App shell, active Checklist Version 1.0 (4 languages), and assigned vehicle profiles are cached locally. Offline submissions are stored in IndexedDB / protected local storage queue.
  • Idempotency Keys: Each offline inspection generates a deterministic key (IDEMP-{vehicleCode}-{empId}-{date}) so retries never create duplicate records.
  • Conflict Resolution & Re-Authorization: During sync, the server re-verifies that the driver was authorized for that vehicle on that date and checks for existing submissions.
  • Sign-out Wipe: Signing out purges sensitive cached employee/inspection payloads from local storage.

3. Identified Business Decisions Confirmed in System Defaults

  • Company Timezone: Set to Asia/Muscat (UTC+04:00) for Al-Ghalbi International Engineering & Contracting LLC (Oman).
  • Safety Critical Items (Auto-Quarantine): Items #1 (Lights), #6 (Seat Belt), #10 (Tyres/Nuts), #15 (Steering), and #17 (Brakes) immediately transition Vehicle Safety Status to Needs Review / Unsafe when marked Fail + High/Critical.
  • OPAL & ROP Compliance: Item #23 (IVMS), #26 (RAS Sticker OPAL), #27 (Mandatory Training Cards), and #30 (Driver ROP License Expiry) are tracked with automated expiry alerts 30 days prior.
